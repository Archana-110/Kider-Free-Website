Vowels are special letters in the alphabet.
There are five vowels:
A, E, I, O, U
These letters help us make words and sounds when we speak. Every word in English has at least one vowel.
For example:
- Apple (A is a vowel)
- Elephant (E is a vowel)
- Ice cream (I is a vowel)
- Orange (O is a vowel)
- Umbrella (U is a vowel)

Consonants are the letters in the alphabet that are not vowels.
There are 21 consonants:
B, C, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y, Z
These letters help us form words along with vowels.
For example:
Ball (B, L are consonants)
Cat (C, T are consonants)
Dog (D, G are consonants)

THIS OR THAT

You use "this" when you are talking about one thing that is near you.
You can say "this" when you can touch it or see it nearby.
Examples :
1. This is my toy. (You are holding it or it’s very close.)
2. This apple is red. (The apple is near you.)

You use "that" when you are talking about one thing that is not near you.
You can say "that" when you see it far, but cannot touch it.
Examples :
1.That is my ball. (The ball is across the room.)
2. That tree is big. (The tree is far away.)

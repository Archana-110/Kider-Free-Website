TYPES OF NOUN

Noun is the name of a person, place, animal, or thing.

A common noun is a general name for a person, place, or thing.
It does not refer to a specific name.
Examples :
1. I saw a dog in the park. (Dog is a common noun.)
2. She goes to school every day. (School is a common noun.)

A proper noun is the specific name of a person, place, or thing.
It always starts with a capital letter.
Examples :
1. Rahul is my best friend. (Rahul is a proper noun.)
2. I live in Mumbai. (Mumbai is a proper noun.)

An abstract noun is a thing that we cannot see, touch, or hold.
It refers to feelings, ideas, qualities, or experiences.
Examples :
1.Happiness is the key to a good life. (Happiness is an abstract noun.)
2.Her kindness made everyone smile. (Kindness is an abstract noun.)

A concrete noun is a thing that we can see, touch, hear, smell, or taste.
It refers to something real and physical.
Examples :
1. I ate an apple. (Apple is a concrete noun because we can see, touch, and taste it.)
2. She is sitting on a chair. (Chair is a concrete noun because we can touch it.)

A collective noun is a word that refers to a group of people, animals, or things as one unit.
Examples :
1. A flock of birds is flying in the sky. (Flock is a collective noun for birds.)
2. She bought a bunch of bananas. (Bunch is a collective noun for bananas.)

A countable noun is a noun that we can count using numbers.
Example :-
1. She bought three oranges. (Oranges are countable.)
2. She bought a bunch of bananas. (Bunch is a collective noun for bananas.)

Refers to things that cannot be counted.
